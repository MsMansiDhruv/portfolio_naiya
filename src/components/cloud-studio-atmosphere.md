Use when working with sky assets, atmosphere continuity, or visibility gates in the portfolio.

**Sky Assets:**
- `sky-base.jpg` - Soft blue-grey with readable clouds
- `clouds-far.png` - Distant soft clouds (PNG alpha)
- `clouds-near.png` - Larger nearer cumulus (PNG alpha)

**Atmosphere Rules:**
- No section color blocks - sky is the continuous film
- Hero dissolves into shared sky (no hard white floor)
- Visibility gate: About/Toolkit mid-scroll must show readable clouds
- Reduced motion: kill parallax, keep base sky visible

**Visibility Gate:**
Screenshot at About or Toolkit mid-scroll:
- [ ] Two distinct cloud masses identifiable
- [ ] Soft blue-grey valleys visible between whites
- [ ] Not a flat off-white slab

**Anti-Wash:**
Never raise white wash opacity to hide seams. Fix assets instead.

**Sections:**
Hero→Toolkit stay transparent - sky is the film
Atmosphere stills only for Contact climax or soft layers
