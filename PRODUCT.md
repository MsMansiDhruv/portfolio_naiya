# Product Truth: Naiya Portfolio

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Product Name**: Naiya Portfolio (Lead Visual Architect & Packaging Designer)
- **Primary Goal**: Showcase elite-tier brand identity, packaging design, digital design systems, and visual architecture through an uncompromised, award-worthy portfolio experience.
- **Core Value Proposition**: Transform high-concept design, physical packaging dielines, and visual branding into an immersive, museum-grade digital showcase.

## 2. Target Audience & Visitor Context
- **Primary Visitors**: Art Directors, Creative Directors, Design Agency Founders, High-End Luxury & Tech Brand Founders.
- **Secondary Visitors**: Senior Design Recruiters, Design Community & Awwwards Judges.
- **Visitor Intent**: Evaluate portfolio quality, design taste, spatial rhythm, execution depth, and attention to detail within 10–15 seconds of landing.

## 3. Aesthetic & Creative Direction
- **Archetype**: Kinetic 3D & GSAP Interactive
- **Visual Features**: Immersive Three.js spatial background canvas, dynamic cursor light-sources, interactive 3D work cards, fluid layout transitions, and high-contrast editorial typography.
- **Tone**: Sophisticated, precise, architectural, confident, understated luxury with high-tech tactile motion.
- **Anti-Patterns**: No SaaS template cliches, no generic cards-inside-cards layouts, no default Inter font, no cheap static gradients.

## 4. Architectural & Quality Constraints
- **Performance**: 60fps smooth scrolling (Lenis), hardware-accelerated transforms (GSAP & Three.js).
- **Accessibility**: High-contrast text compliance, screen-reader semantic landmarks, motion fallback support (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Responsiveness**: Fluid layout across ultra-wide, laptop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.

## 5. Build Path & Workflow Settings
- **Build Strategy**: `comp-first` (Generate high-fidelity visual comps/mockups for key sections first before implementing code).
- **Status**: Impeccable initialized (`/impeccable init` completed).
